How to Use the Porkchop Plot

What It Shows
A "porkchop" (or "launch window") plot displays transfer opportunities between two bodies. Each cell represents a different launch date and flight time combination, color-coded by the Δv required.
How to Read It
X-axis: Departure date (left = earlier)
Y-axis: Time of flight (top = shorter journey)
Colors: Blue/Green = efficient, Red = expensive
Finding Good Windows
Look for "valleys" or blue/green areas—these represent efficient transfer windows. Avoid red regions (expensive). The bluest cell is the optimal transfer.
How to Plan
1. Generate the plot
2. Find a blue/green valley
3. Click a cell to see exact timing
4. Check "Next Transfer Window" in main panel
Clicking Cells
Click any cell to inspect its details: exact departure date, arrival date, and Δv required. Use this to fine-tune your launch plan and see if a particular window fits your schedule.
